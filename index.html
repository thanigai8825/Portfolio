Design philosophy

Start with the decision
someone needs to make.

Understand the business

Before I draw, I ask what needs to change. Who is this for? What do they need to understand, choose or do?

Make it tangible

A sketch, a prototype or a few seconds of motion gives us something specific to discuss. That’s where the useful questions begin.

Follow it through

I carry the idea into the details, from responsive layouts and states to the files your team will use next.
